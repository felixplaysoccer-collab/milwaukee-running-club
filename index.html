Welcome to the Milwaukee Running Club! Learn about local runners, races, and training tips.

Training Tips

Running is a great way to stay healthy and active. Before running, make sure to warm up so your body is ready to exercise. Start with a comfortable pace.

Remember to drink water and take breaks when needed. If you are new to running, begin with short distances. You can slowly increase how far you run each week.

Upcoming Races

Milwaukee has different running events throughout the year. Some races are short, like a 5K, while others are longer. People with different experience levels can participate.

Joining a race is a fun way to meet other runners. It can also help you set a goal for your training. Check local event websites for race dates and registration details.

Featured Runner

Our featured runner is Alex, who enjoys running around Milwaukee. Alex started with short runs around the neighborhood. Over time, running became part of a weekly routine.

Alex likes running with friends because it makes exercise more enjoyable. The goal is to keep improving little by little. Staying consistent has made a big difference.
